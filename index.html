Bank Statement → Tally Import

Designed for CA articleship trainees, small accounting practices and bookkeepers.

💡 For the best result, upload the original file from your bank (the PDF, Excel or CSV you downloaded from net banking or the bank app), not a scanned, photographed, screenshotted, edited or redacted copy. Original files hold the exact dates, amounts and narrations. Scanned or redacted copies have to be read by OCR, which can misread dates and narrations.
Statement not reading properly? Convert it to CSV yourself with Claude / ChatGPT etc.
  1. Open an AI chat of your choice and attach your bank statement.
  2. Copy the prompt below and send it.
  3. Download the CSV it gives you and check it against the statement.
  4. Upload that CSV file in step 1 above.
Opens claude.ai in a new tab with the prompt filled in. Attach your statement there and press send; you stay signed in to your own account.

ℹ Before the final upload to Tally: a ledger-wise summary of every ledger this import will post to (entries, debit and credit totals, and whether each ledger is new or already in Tally) is available on the Create ledgers page. Please check it first.

In Tally: Gateway → Import → Data (or Alt+O) → choose the XML. Every ledger used must exist in Tally. Use Create missing ledgers to add any that don\'t; they are written into the same XML, ahead of the vouchers, so one import does both. Also make sure the voucher dates fall inside the company's financial year / books-beginning date.
