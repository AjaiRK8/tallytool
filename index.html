Use AI only if this PDF cannot be read reliably within this tool. Off by default. The tool always tries its own PDF text reading and OCR first and gives you the best verified result it can. If they cannot fully verify the result, AI is used only if you tick this. If you leave it unticked, you will be asked at that point whether to use AI or continue with the current data.
⚠ Confidentiality warning

If AI is used, images of your statement pages are sent over the internet to Anthropic's Claude AI using your own API key. Pages may show the account holder's name, account number, address and every transaction.

This tool and its author (Ajai R Krishnan) do not receive or store your data, but are not responsible for how it is handled once sent, or for any confidentiality, client-consent or data-protection duty you owe (for example professional secrecy or privacy laws). Handling is governed by your own agreement with Anthropic.

If you leave this unticked, or do not agree, nothing is sent anywhere: you still get the best automatic result in the preview, where you can view and edit narrations, ledgers and types and check the flagged rows.
