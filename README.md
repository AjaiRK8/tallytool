This free tool converts a bank statement into a Tally XML file, so you do not have to type each entry in Tally. It works with PDF, scanned PDF, Excel, CSV and photo statements, and everything is read on your own device.

Upload the statement. Use the original PDF, Excel or CSV from net banking where possible. It gives the most accurate dates, amounts and narrations.
Set the company and bank ledger. Optionally load your Tally ledger list for type-ahead and checks.
Review the entries. Each row is checked against the running balance of the statement. Rows that do not add up are flagged. Assign ledgers using your own rules.
Create missing ledgers. The tool lists ledgers that are not yet in Tally and gives a ledger-wise summary.
Export the Tally XML. In Tally, import the file through Import Data. Compare the entries with the original statement before finalising your books.
Who is it for? CA articleship trainees, small accounting practices and bookkeepers who enter bank statements into Tally often.
